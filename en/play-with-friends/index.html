Three steps to kick-off

  1. 1Create a room

    Tap “Play now”. Under “Mode” pick “With Friends”, tap “Play” and then “Create room”.

  2. 2Send the link

    You get a link, a QR code and a four-character room code. Send the link through your messenger, or let your friend scan the QR code.

  3. 3Kick off

    Your friend opens the link and lands right in your lobby. Each of you picks a head, you choose the arena and rules, and you kick off.

What you need

  • a current browser on a phone, tablet or PC,
  • an internet connection. The game connects your devices directly; if your network does not allow that, the connection runs through a relay server,
  • no account, no app and no money.

Your rules

As the host, you decide how you play: arena, obstacles, match length (60 s, 90 s, 120 s or first to 3 goals or 5 goals), speed, shot power, bounce, ball, goal and field size, gravity, power-ups and special shots.

After the final whistle, go straight into a rematch.

Two players on one device

Sitting next to each other? Then you don't need a link: like in classic head soccer games, you can share one device. Pick “Two Players” and play on one keyboard, with two controllers or on a split touchscreen.

FAQ

Does my friend need to sign up or install anything?

No. They open the link in their browser and they are in your lobby.

Can I play from my phone against someone on a PC?

Yes. Headstars runs in the browser on every device, and you meet in the same lobby.

What if the link doesn't arrive?

Your friend also picks “With Friends” in the game and enters the four-character room code under “Your buddy's code”.

What happens if the connection drops?

The game pauses and waits briefly for the other side. If it comes back, you continue right where you left off.

Can I play against strangers too?

Yes, in Casual (from level 2) and Ranked (from level 5, account needed). More under game modes.

Does playing online cost anything?

No. Headstars is free, online too.
