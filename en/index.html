Head it, shoot it, slide in

Two players, one ball, two goals. How the ball flies depends on the moment you hit it: a ball at rest goes up in an arc, a ball at head height flies low, and in the air you hit a volley.

On the ground you slide tackle, in the air you come down with a ground pound, and the higher you are, the harder it lands. Get the timing right and you head the ball straight out of the air into the goal.

FAQ

Is Headstars free?

Yes. Headstars costs nothing. You earn coins and gems for new heads, balls and arenas by playing. There are no real-money purchases.

Do I need to install anything or create an account?

No. Headstars runs in your browser. On first start the game creates a guest account, with no email and no password. If you want to save your progress or keep playing on another device, link the account to your email address. Ranked and the leaderboards require that.

Does Headstars work on my phone?

Yes. On phones and tablets you play with touch controls, best in landscape. If your browser offers it, you can install Headstars like an app (Settings → Install app).

Can I play with a controller?

Yes. The button layout is in the table under “Controls”. When two of you play on one device, each player can use their own controller.
