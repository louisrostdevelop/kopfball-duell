2. Overview

Kopfball Duell is a browser game. You can play without registering. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or social-media plugins. Fonts and libraries are served from our own website, not from third parties.

3. Data stored on your device

The game stores its state in your browser's local storage: progress, unlocked items, settings (sound, language, graphics), and, if the online backend is active, a login session token and match reports that could not be sent yet. This is necessary to provide the game (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings or in the game under Settings → Delete progress.

4. Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open the page, your browser sends technical data (IP address, time, requested file, browser type) to GitHub's servers, which GitHub processes to deliver the page and protect its service. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub may process data in the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

5. Player account and game server

If the online features are active, the game uses Supabase (Supabase, Inc.; servers in the EU, Frankfurt, Germany) as its game server. When you first start the game, a guest account is created automatically. We process:

  • Account: a random user ID; if you save your progress: your e-mail address and a password hash (we never see your password).
  • Game data: player name, level, coins and gems, owned items, head levels, season pass, tasks, achievements, match results, ratings, leaderboard entries, friends list and friend requests, time of last activity.
  • Technical data: IP address and request metadata in the server logs of Supabase, kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting.

Purpose: providing the game, saving progress across devices, fair matchmaking, leaderboards, preventing cheating and abuse (for example daily limits and plausibility checks). Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in a fair and secure game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Visible to others: your player name, level, league and rating, and leaderboard scores are visible to other players. Friends see when you were last online.

Guest accounts without purchases are deleted automatically after 90 days of inactivity.

6. Protection against automated sign-ups

To prevent bots from creating accounts, we may use Cloudflare Turnstile (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) when a guest account is created. Turnstile processes your IP address and browser characteristics to tell humans from bots. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in protecting the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. Online matches with other players

Online matches run directly between the two players' browsers (peer-to-peer, WebRTC). To establish the connection, the game uses the PeerJS signalling server (0.peerjs.com) and connection-helper servers (STUN by Google and Cloudflare; TURN relay by PeerJS). During a match your opponent's browser receives your IP address, as with any direct connection. If a direct connection is not possible, data is relayed through the TURN server. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If you do not want this, do not play online matches.

8. Purchases

Payments are processed by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland). You enter your payment details directly with Stripe; we never receive your card or bank details. We receive and store the payment ID, product, amount, currency, time, and status (paid, refunded, disputed). Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). Purchase records are kept for 10 years as required by German commercial and tax law (§ 147 AO, § 257 HGB); if you delete your account, they are kept without any link to your player profile. Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.
